---
title: GRILLING
---
Agents file the decisions they need from the human with kabane_ask, and kabane_questions lists the open ones with their choices, the asker's recommendation (★) and why. When the human asks you to grill them, to go through the open questions, or anything like it, follow the grill skill at {{extension}}/templates/grill/SKILL.md: read the questions for the scope, or for the issue they name, say how many are open across how many issues, then take them one at a time, highest-priority issue first. For each one, restate it in a line, give the choices and the recommended one with its why, and check what the code or the tracker can answer before you ask. Then ask that one question and wait for the reply: through your harness's own choice prompt whenever it has one, the choices as its options and the recommended one first, and in plain text, ending your turn on it, only where it has none. Just before recording, re-read that issue with kabane_questions and its id, because a handle can shift when sync brings in an earlier question: if the handle no longer shows the same question text, use the one that does and tell the human what moved. Record a choice with kabane_answer and relayed: true, which credits the decision to the human rather than to you, with their own words as reasoning; "skip" leaves it open; "withdraw" is kabane_answer with withdraw: true, relayed: true and their reason. When the human's decision does not fit the question's choices, as two picks on a single choice do, never squeeze it into the nearest one: reshape the question with kabane_ask and replaces set to its handle, with choices that hold what was concluded, which withdraws the old one and keeps the issue waiting, then record the decision on the new handle with kabane_answer and relayed: true. Say when an issue moves to next, and end with a one-line tally. Only the human answers: never record an answer they did not give, the recommended one included.

The same skill runs in any coding session that has the kabane MCP. When the human asks how, point them at that path. When they ask you to install it in this project, copy SKILL.md unchanged to .agents/skills/grill/SKILL.md under the dispatch skill's rule: when neither .agents/skills/grill/ nor a real .claude/skills/grill/ directory exists, the request is the go-ahead; an existing .agents/skills/grill/ gets a diff and waits for a yes; a real .claude/skills/grill/ directory gets the offer to move it and waits for a yes; and the .claude/skills/grill link to ../../.agents/skills/grill follows the dispatch skill's link rule.
