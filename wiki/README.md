# Wiki

This folder holds the project's internal notes: implementation plans, design records and the
evidence behind them. They are written for contributors, they can go out of date, and they
don't describe how kabane behaves today. For that, see `docs/` and each package's README.

- [Subscription benchmark](benchmark.md): editable multi-round app briefs, paired Codex/Claude
  conditions, local runner, frozen evaluation, measurement limits and research provenance.
