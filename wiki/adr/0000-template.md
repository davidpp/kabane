# NNNN: The decision, as one sentence

- Status: accepted | superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Issue: the issue the decision came from
- Decided by: David, or the agent that decided while he was away

## Context

The situation that forced a choice, and the constraints that applied to it.

## Decision

What was chosen and the reason on record. Point at the code or the check that holds the detail,
and do not copy it here.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it leaves undecided.

## Alternatives rejected

One line each, with the reason. Or name this section "Replaceable by" and say what would
replace the decision, and what would have to happen first.
